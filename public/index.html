How to Play Yu-Gi-Oh! Master Duel Web

1. Follow the Gold & Cyan Playable Frames

Cards in your hand, field, GY, or Extra Deck with legal plays show a crisp gold or cyan frame. Tap or click any highlighted card to view actions and choose a zone.

2. Extra Deck Summoning

When valid materials are on the field (Synchro, Xyz, Fusion, or Link), the Extra Deck pile lights up — tap it to summon your boss monster.

3. Authentic Chain Link Priority

When your opponent activates a card and you hold a Quick Effect or Hand Trap (such as Ash Blossom or Effect Veiler), the Chain Bar prompts you to respond or pass.

4. Multiplayer Across Devices

On the same Wi-Fi, let your friend scan the Lobby QR code or open the LAN URL, create a room, and duel live with synchronized OCG rules.
